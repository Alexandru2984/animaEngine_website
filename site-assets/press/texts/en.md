# animaEngine — Animated characters that live on your desktop

Animated characters that live on your desktop: they walk, bounce, follow each other, say things and remind you of breaks. Linux and Windows. No network, open source.

They walk, bounce, follow each other and play tag over your windows — or behind them. Tap one and it says hello. Clicks go straight through to your work.

## What it does

- **Any picture comes alive** — Drop a PNG, GIF, WebP or a short MP4 on the screen, or paste a picture: it becomes a character.
- **A life of their own** — Walking, wandering, bouncing, following the cursor or each other, playing tag — or a small script you write.
- **Around your day** — Reminders to take a break or drink water, a focus timer, bedtime, and a welcome back when you return.
- **Out of your way** — Clicks pass through. They step aside for full-screen games and films, and hold still while you are away.
- **Scenes to share** — Save what is on screen under a name, switch by the time of day, and share a scene or a character as one file.
- **Light** — Next to no CPU while nothing moves, thirty frames a second if you like, and on a Linux laptop the NVIDIA GPU left asleep.
- **For everyone** — Ten languages, screen readers, every action from the keyboard, high-contrast themes.
- **Private by design** — It makes no network connection at all. Nothing about you or your desktop leaves your computer.
